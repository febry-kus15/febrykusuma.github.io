The brief

Kura-Kura Bali is evolving from a masterplan vision into a delivery programme. The central planning question was whether existing and planned infrastructure could support the updated development quantum, and what needed to be safeguarded now for long-term growth.

The work established a coordinated planning basis across land use, gross floor area, population and five development phases. Demand calculations were then tested against existing capacities to identify gaps, risks, dependencies and opportunities for scalable delivery.

Infrastructure was treated not as a downstream engineering exercise, but as a spatial framework that shapes land allocation, resilience, phasing and investment readiness.

From development data to infrastructure decisions

The coordinated model connected planning inputs with water supply, wastewater, power, solid waste and telecommunications. It also provided a basis for wider drainage, coastal resilience, road access and utility-corridor discussions.
